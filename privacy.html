Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how the LINGRID mobile app ("the app") and its developer, Beyda Dönmez ("we", "us"), handle information. The app has no account system and no servers of our own. It uses advertising and consent services from Google, and purchases, iCloud sync and Game Center from Apple, each under the terms described below.

1. Who is responsible

Beyda Dönmez, the developer and publisher of the app, is responsible for the app and for this policy. You can reach us at lingrid.sandbox@outlook.com.

2. Data stored only on your device

To make the game work, the app stores the following on your device, in the app's own storage: your puzzle progress and best scores, daily puzzle results and streak, your diamond balance and its transaction history, whether you have purchased Remove Ads, which colour themes you have unlocked, the free-puzzle allowance for the day, puzzles you have started, and your sound, music, colour theme and appearance settings. This data does not leave your device through the app, except as described in "iCloud sync" below, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the app, unless a copy has been kept in your iCloud account through iCloud sync. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) may also include it under Apple's control.

3. Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app sends the following to Apple so that leaderboards and achievements work: your daily puzzle score together with that day's puzzle number, your all-time total score, and your achievement progress. Your Game Center nickname and scores can be visible to other players on leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; you can manage or turn it off in your device's Settings. The app uses your Game Center identity only to show whether you are signed in; it does not store it or send it anywhere else.

4. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The app shows a short interstitial ad only when you leave the results screen of a puzzle you have just finished — never during your first three finished puzzles, and never more than once every three minutes — and an optional "rewarded" ad you can choose to watch from the diamond shop for +10 diamonds, up to three times a day. These ads are served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK. To request, show and measure them, Google and its advertising partners may process: an approximate, coarse location estimated from your IP address; non-user performance data such as app launch time, crash reports and hang rate, which is used for diagnostics and may also be used to help decide which ads to show, including by sharing it with other companies that display ads; a device identifier — your device's advertising identifier where allowed, or another app- or developer-bound identifier — used for third-party advertising, our own advertising and analytics; and information about which ads you have seen and how you use the app, such as app launches. This list is Google's own disclosure of what its SDK can collect, not our choice; see "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" and Google's Privacy Policy. Before showing ads to a user in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message (the "UMP" form) so you can choose whether to allow personalised advertising.

5. App Tracking Transparency and non-personalised ads

On iOS, before the app first requests an ad it asks for your permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework, shown in Turkish and in English. If you decline tracking, if you have turned tracking off for the app in your device's Settings, or if UMP consent has not been given, the app does not use your device's advertising identifier for tracking and only requests non-personalised ads — ads chosen without a profile built from your activity in other apps or websites. Non-personalised ads still use the Google Mobile Ads SDK data described above (for example, coarse location and a non-tracking device identifier), because Google's SDK needs that data to request, serve, measure and protect any ad, personalised or not. You can change your ATT choice at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; where the EEA/UK/Switzerland consent message applies, you can change your choice from the link Google provides inside that message.

6. Purchases through Apple

The app offers in-app purchases — a one-time Remove Ads purchase and consumable diamond packs. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit); we do not run our own store and we never receive your payment details, card number or billing address — Apple processes those under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation of which product you bought and that the purchase succeeded, and finishes each transaction on your device. See our Terms of Use for what each purchase gives you and how Restore Purchases works.

7. iCloud sync (Apple)

When iCloud is turned on for the app on your device, the app uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage, part of your own iCloud account, to keep the following in sync across your devices and through a reinstall on the same iCloud account: your diamond balance and its transaction history, whether you have purchased Remove Ads, which colour themes you have unlocked, and your daily streak. This storage is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we have no server of our own and cannot see this data. If iCloud is off, or you are signed out of iCloud, this data stays only on your device as described in "Data stored only on your device" above, and is lost if you delete the app. To stop this syncing, open your device's Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn iCloud off for the app (or turn off iCloud Drive generally).

8. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, precise location, contacts or notifications. It asks for permission to use your device's advertising identifier under Apple's App Tracking Transparency, only to support the advertising described above; you can decline this at any time (see "App Tracking Transparency and non-personalised ads").

9. Network

All puzzles, sounds and images are included in the app. The app connects to the internet for: Apple Game Center, as described above; Google's advertising and consent services, to request and show ads and to gather advertising consent; and Apple's App Store/StoreKit and iCloud services, for purchases and sync. The app shows these legal texts without a connection. When you open their web version, your browser loads it from GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., which processes technical request data such as your IP address to deliver the page. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

10. Children

LINGRID is a general-audience word-learning game; we do not direct its marketing at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A user who has not given tracking consent, of any age, is only ever shown non-personalised ads, as described above. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

11. Your rights

We hold no personal data of our own beyond what is described above, so there is little for us to access, correct or delete directly. You can delete the data stored on your device by deleting the app; this does not delete a copy kept in your iCloud account, which you control from your device's Settings app under your name, then iCloud. For your Apple ID, Game Center or purchase data, contact Apple. For the advertising data Google's SDK has processed about you, use Google's own controls at myadcenter.google.com, or the choices described in "App Tracking Transparency and non-personalised ads" above. You can still write to us with any privacy question.

12. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
